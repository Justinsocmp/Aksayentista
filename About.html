Who We Are

The Supreme Student Learner Government (SSLG) of Angeles City Science High School is the highest governing student body dedicated to the welfare and representation of the student populace. We serve as the bridge between the students and the school administration.

Our core focus is to foster a proactive, inclusive, and innovative school environment. Through various programs, initiatives, and community engagements, we aim to hone the leadership skills of every ACSci student while upholding academic excellence.

Our Mission

To empower the student body through active representation, transparent governance, and the execution of meaningful projects that promote holistic development, social responsibility, and academic excellence within the campus and beyond.

Our Vision

We envision a united, dynamic, and forward-thinking student community where every learner is equipped with the leadership skills and values necessary to become catalysts for positive change in the modern world.
